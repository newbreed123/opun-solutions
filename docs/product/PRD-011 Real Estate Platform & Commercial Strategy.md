# PRD-011 Real Estate Platform & Commercial Strategy

Version: 1.0

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Vision

Build the most intelligent growth platform for modern real estate professionals.

Opzix combines MLS-integrated property experiences, AI, analytics, automation, scheduling, community intelligence, and brokerage operations into one connected platform.

Rather than selling websites, Opzix sells an operating platform that helps agents and brokerages generate more opportunities, improve customer experience, and run their business more efficiently.

## Product Mission

Deliver a reusable platform that can be configured for:

- Individual agents
- Luxury agents
- Teams
- Boutique brokerages
- Enterprise brokerages

without rebuilding the product each time.

## Positioning

Opzix does not compete with Fiverr, local web agencies, generic IDX templates, or one-off website vendors.

Opzix is positioned as:

The AI-Powered Real Estate Growth Platform.

## Core Philosophy

Every customer receives the same platform foundation.

Plans unlock:

- Additional modules
- Automation
- AI capabilities
- Customization
- Operational tools
- Support

not completely different products.

## Customer Segments

### Launch

New agents who need professional online presence, MLS search, lead capture, scheduling, AI assistance, hosting, and maintenance.

Goal: start generating business.

### Growth

Established agents who need more seller leads, SEO, community pages, automation, analytics, and lead routing.

Goal: scale production.

### Elite

Top-producing agents, luxury agents, and small teams that need custom branding, AI, community intelligence, market reports, advanced analytics, home valuation, seller funnels, and buyer funnels.

Goal: differentiate from competitors.

Brittany can be used as an in-progress reference design when discussing this tier.

### Enterprise

Teams, brokerages, and large organizations that need brokerage workflows, multiple agents, operational dashboards, internal AI, CRM integrations, reporting, and custom APIs.

Goal: run the brokerage on Opzix.

## Commercial Strategy

Opzix is not selling pages. Opzix is selling business growth.

Pricing reflects:

- Platform
- Support
- Innovation
- AI
- Hosting
- Compliance
- Continuous improvements

Revenue model:

- One-time onboarding
- Monthly platform subscription

## Recommended Pricing

- Launch: $750-$1,250 setup, $249/month
- Growth: $1,500-$2,500 setup, $499/month
- Elite: starting around $5,000+ setup, $899-$1,499/month
- Enterprise: custom proposal, no public pricing

## Product Principles

Every new feature must answer:

- Is it reusable across multiple agents?
- Does it strengthen the platform rather than one implementation?
- Does it improve recurring customer value?
- Can it become a configurable module?
- Does it increase customer growth or operational efficiency?

If the answer is no, reconsider building it.

## Reference Design Strategy

BrittanyFlannigan.com is an in-progress example implementation and reference design.

It is not one custom website.

Every feature configured for Brittany should become a reusable platform module unless there is a strong reason for customer-specific configuration.
