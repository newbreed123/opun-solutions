# PRD-010 Scheduling & Client Engagement

Version: 1.0

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Purpose

Scheduling and client engagement connect user intent to timely follow-up, meeting preparation, and operational visibility.

## Goals

- Capture appointment intent from pages, forms, and Zora conversations.
- Preserve context for the team receiving the appointment.
- Send useful confirmations and reminders.
- Track engagement outcomes from booking through completion.
- Reuse engagement workflows across real estate, service, and ecommerce implementations.

## Core Capabilities

- Appointment intake
- Source attribution
- Service-requested context
- Confirmation emails
- Reminder emails
- Meeting link support
- Internal notifications
- Dashboard visibility

## Real Estate Use Cases

- Buyer consultation
- Seller consultation
- Home valuation discussion
- Listing inquiry follow-up
- Relocation consultation
- Brokerage strategy session

## Metrics

- Appointment requests
- Confirmed appointments
- Reminder delivery
- No-show reduction
- Source-to-booking conversion
- Zora-to-booking conversion
