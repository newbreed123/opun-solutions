# PRD-002 Zora AI

Version: 1.0

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Purpose

Zora is the AI layer across Opzix. It should act as a growth consultant, guided assistant, intake system, and operational intelligence surface depending on context.

## Goals

- Answer visitor questions with useful business context.
- Guide prospects toward the next appropriate action.
- Qualify leads without creating friction.
- Support industry-specific journeys such as ecommerce audits and real estate buyer/seller guidance.
- Feed sanitized learning signals into founder and product dashboards.

## Core Modes

- General growth consultant
- Ecommerce audit explainer
- Real estate buyer assistant
- Real estate seller assistant
- Scheduling assistant
- Operational assistant

## Product Rules

- Zora should not behave like a generic FAQ widget.
- Zora should answer direct questions before forcing a CTA.
- Zora should adapt to the confirmed business type or industry.
- Real estate capabilities should be reusable across agents, teams, and brokerages.

## Metrics

- Conversations started
- Qualified leads
- CTA clicks after Zora
- Strategy sessions booked
- Unresolved questions
- Low-confidence fallback rate
- Intent and concept distribution
