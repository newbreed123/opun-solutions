# PRD-001 Platform Vision

Version: 1.0

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Vision

Build an AI-powered growth platform that helps businesses connect customer experience, lead generation, analytics, automation, scheduling, and operational visibility.

Opzix should be positioned as a reusable operating platform, not a collection of one-off websites.

## Product Mission

Deliver reusable modules that can be configured for customer and industry needs without rebuilding the product each time.

Core customer categories include:

- Ecommerce brands
- Service businesses
- Real estate professionals
- Future industry verticals

## Platform Pillars

- Zora AI
- Analytics
- Scheduling
- CRM and follow-up
- Automation
- Dashboards
- Website and experience engine
- Diagnostics and audit tools
- Integrations

## Product Principles

- Every major feature should improve customer growth, operational efficiency, or platform intelligence.
- Industry implementations should share a common platform foundation.
- Vertical-specific modules should adapt shared capabilities to a real customer journey.
- Real estate reference work should be described accurately as in-progress platform demonstration, reference design, or example implementation until launch status and measurable outcomes are available.

## Success Metrics

- Qualified leads generated
- Appointments booked
- Conversion rate
- AI conversations
- Customer retention
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Feature adoption
- Dashboard engagement
