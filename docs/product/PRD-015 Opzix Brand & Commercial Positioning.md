# PRD-015 Opzix Brand & Commercial Positioning

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Purpose

Reposition Opzix for long-term growth before significant investment in Google Ads and outbound sales.

This is a messaging sprint, not a redesign, feature sprint, navigation sprint, or animation sprint.

## Company Position

Opzix is not a web design agency, IDX website provider, chatbot company, or marketing agency.

Opzix is an AI-powered business platform company.

Opzix designs connected business platforms that combine customer experience, automation, analytics, AI, integrations, and operational workflows.

## Official Company Narrative

Do not tell the story as "we built a Realtor website" or "we have one Realtor client."

Tell the story this way:

For years, Opzix has focused on building modern business platforms, AI systems, automation, analytics, integrations, and customer experiences.

Today, that same expertise powers connected solutions for licensed real estate professionals through approved MLS-integrated technology.

## Messaging Rules

- The homepage should emphasize business growth, connected platforms, AI, automation, operational visibility, and industry expertise.
- Real Estate should be presented as one industry served, not the primary company identity.
- Use platform language, business growth language, and technology company positioning.
- Avoid agency language, website-first language, project-based language, cheap website positioning, and generic claims such as "best websites" or "industry leader."
- Technology should be translated into what it does, why it matters, and the business outcome.

## Real Estate Positioning

Position Opzix as a technology company bringing modern software platform thinking to the real estate industry.

Recommended story:

Real estate deserves better technology.

For years, businesses have benefited from modern AI, analytics, automation, and connected customer platforms.

Opzix now brings those same principles to licensed real estate professionals through approved MLS-integrated technology.

## Brittany Positioning

Do not build credibility around one implementation.

Do not imply Brittany is the flagship customer, first customer, biggest customer, or proof of years of production success.

Use Brittany only as a product preview, platform demonstration, example implementation, or reference design.

If the implementation is still under construction, state that accurately.

## MLS Positioning

Do not market "we have MLS access."

Use customer-facing language such as IDX Property Search, Buyer Experiences, Seller Journeys, Community Intelligence, Lead Management, and Business Growth.

When needed, describe the capability as approved MLS-integrated technology supporting modern real estate experiences.

## Technology Philosophy

Most agencies build websites.

Opzix builds business platforms.

Every website, AI assistant, analytics dashboard, scheduling system, CRM, and operational workflow should function as one connected platform.

## Google Ads Positioning

Future campaigns should target business problems, not cheap websites or template websites.

Useful campaign directions include IDX website, real estate technology, AI for Realtors, real estate CRM, lead generation for Realtors, real estate automation, and MLS website.

Position the offer around business growth, platform value, customer experience, and operational efficiency.

## Final Goal

When someone leaves Opzix they should think:

"This company builds serious business platforms."

Not:

"This company builds websites."
