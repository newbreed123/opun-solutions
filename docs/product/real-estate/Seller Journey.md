# Seller Journey

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Purpose

The Seller Journey captures homeowner intent through valuation, preparation, local expertise, AI guidance, scheduling, and follow-up.

## Goals

- Create stronger seller lead paths than a generic contact form.
- Help sellers understand timing, preparation, and consultation options.
- Preserve seller intent and property context for follow-up.
- Support higher-value seller funnels for Growth and Elite platform paths.

## Journey Stages

1. Visitor lands on a seller, valuation, or community page.
2. Visitor reviews local expertise, market context, or preparation guidance.
3. Visitor asks Zora a seller question.
4. Visitor shares property, timeline, goal, or valuation context.
5. Visitor submits a valuation or consultation request.
6. Visitor books a seller consultation.
7. Lead context enters follow-up and dashboard workflows.

## Core Modules

- Seller funnel
- Home valuation flow
- Community Intelligence
- Zora seller assistant
- Native scheduling
- Analytics
- CRM and automation

## Product Rules

- Valuation language should be framed carefully and avoid unsupported guarantees.
- Seller forms should balance conversion with qualification.
- Market reports and community intelligence should support trust and expertise.
- Seller features should become reusable platform modules, not Brittany-only logic.

## Metrics

- Seller inquiries
- Home valuation requests
- Zora seller conversations
- Seller consultation bookings
- Seller source attribution
- Follow-up completion
