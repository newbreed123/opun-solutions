# Real Estate Platform Pricing

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Positioning

Opzix is not selling pages. Opzix is selling business growth.

The offer should be framed as "Choose Your Growth Platform," not "Plans & Pricing."

## Revenue Model

- One-time onboarding
- Monthly platform subscription

Recurring revenue reflects:

- Hosting
- Security
- MLS maintenance
- Platform improvements
- AI improvements
- Analytics
- Support
- Feature updates
- Compliance updates
- Performance monitoring

## Platform Paths

### Essentials: Build Your Foundation

Ideal customer: new agents.

Setup: $750-$1,250

Monthly: $249/month

Core value:

- Professional website foundation
- MLS-ready structure
- Lead capture
- Scheduling
- AI assistant
- Hosting and maintenance

### Professional: Generate More Buyers and Sellers

Ideal customer: growing agents.

Setup: $1,500-$2,500

Monthly: $499/month

Core value:

- Everything in Essentials
- SEO
- Seller funnels
- Buyer funnels
- Automation
- Analytics
- CRM
- Lead routing

### Performance: Dominate Your Market

Ideal customer: top-producing agents, luxury agents, and small teams.

Setup: starting around $5,000+

Monthly: $899-$1,499/month

Core value:

- Everything in Professional
- Community intelligence
- Market reports
- Advanced AI
- Advanced analytics
- Operational intelligence
- Custom workflows
- Brand customization

### Brokerage: Power Your Brokerage

Ideal customer: teams, brokerages, and large organizations.

Setup: custom proposal

Monthly: contact sales

Core value:

- Everything in Performance
- Brokerage back office
- Operational dashboards
- Internal AI
- Team analytics
- Role-based workflows
- Custom integrations
- Custom deployment

## CTA

The primary CTA should be "Book a Strategy Session."

The goal is to qualify needs, explain outcomes, and scope the right platform path before proposal.
