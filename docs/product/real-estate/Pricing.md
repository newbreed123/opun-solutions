# Real Estate Platform Pricing

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Positioning

Opzix is not selling pages. Opzix is selling business growth.

The offer should be framed as "Choose Your Growth Platform," not "Plans & Pricing."

## Revenue Model

- Setup fee
- Monthly subscription

Recurring revenue reflects:

- Hosting
- Security
- MLS maintenance
- Platform improvements
- AI improvements
- Analytics
- Support
- Feature updates
- Compliance updates
- Performance monitoring

## Platform Paths

### Launch: Start Getting More Calls

Ideal customer: independent agents starting their business.

Setup Fee: $500

Monthly: $200/month

Core value:

- MLS Home Search
- AI Assistant
- Lead capture
- CRM
- Community Guides

### Growth: Turn Interest Into Clients

Ideal customer: agents with steady traffic and lead flow.

Setup Fee: $750

Monthly: $275/month

Core value:

- Everything in Launch
- AI Lead Advisor
- Marketing Automation
- Lead Intelligence
- Advanced Analytics

### Performance: Scale Your Business With Confidence

Ideal customer: high-performing agents and teams.

Setup Fee: Custom

Monthly: Custom

Core value:

- Everything in Growth
- Market Intelligence
- AI Content
- Advanced Reporting
- Custom workflows

### Brokerage: Power Your Entire Brokerage

Ideal customer: teams, brokerages, and large organizations.

Setup Fee: Custom

Monthly: Custom

Core value:

- Team Management
- Agent Routing
- Brokerage Dashboard
- Reporting
- Multi-Agent Platform
