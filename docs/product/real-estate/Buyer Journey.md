# Buyer Journey

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Purpose

The Buyer Journey connects property discovery, community research, AI guidance, lead capture, scheduling, and follow-up.

## Goals

- Help buyers clarify needs and next steps.
- Connect listing and community interest to a consultation path.
- Preserve buyer context for the agent or team.
- Track engagement across search, AI, forms, and booking.

## Journey Stages

1. Visitor lands on a property, search, or community page.
2. Visitor explores listings or local information.
3. Visitor asks Zora a buyer question.
4. Visitor identifies budget, timing, location, or property preferences.
5. Visitor submits an inquiry or consultation request.
6. Visitor books a buyer consultation.
7. Lead context enters follow-up and dashboard workflows.

## Core Modules

- IDX Module
- Community Intelligence
- Zora buyer assistant
- Lead capture
- Native scheduling
- Analytics
- CRM and automation

## Product Rules

- Buyer capture should ask for useful routing context without unnecessary friction.
- Property inquiry should include listing or community context.
- Zora should avoid pretending to replace licensed agent advice.
- Buyer features should be reusable across future real estate deployments.

## Metrics

- Buyer inquiries
- Property engagement
- Zora buyer conversations
- Buyer consultation bookings
- Booking completion rate
- Buyer source attribution
