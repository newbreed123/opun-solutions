# Community Intelligence

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Purpose

Community Intelligence helps real estate professionals demonstrate local authority and guide buyers or sellers through neighborhood context.

## Goals

- Create useful local content that supports search, trust, and conversion.
- Help buyers understand lifestyle fit.
- Help sellers see market and neighborhood expertise.
- Feed Zora with structured community context.
- Support reusable community page templates and data models.

## Content Areas

- Neighborhoods
- Lifestyle
- Schools
- Restaurants
- Parks
- Healthcare
- Shopping
- Commute
- Market insights
- Relocation context

## Product Rules

- Community content should support real decision-making, not thin SEO filler.
- Market-specific details should be configuration/content, not hardcoded logic.
- Claims about schools, safety, market performance, and compliance-sensitive areas should be carefully sourced or framed.
- The module should support future agents in different markets.

## Metrics

- Community page views
- Local search traffic
- Zora community questions
- Buyer inquiries from community pages
- Seller inquiries from community pages
- Consultation bookings from community pages
