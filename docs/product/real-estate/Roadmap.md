# Real Estate Platform Roadmap

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Phase 1: Current

Focus: reusable real estate platform launch foundation.

Priorities:

- BrittanyFlannigan.com as an in-progress reference design
- MLS Grid integration planning
- IDX module planning
- Community Intelligence
- Native scheduling
- Zora AI real estate behavior
- Founder Dashboard visibility
- Buyer and seller journey foundations

## Phase 2: Public Launch

Focus: package the real estate offer for new customers.

Priorities:

- Launch platform path
- Growth platform path
- Elite platform path
- Real estate marketing pages
- Choose Your Growth Platform page
- Subscription onboarding planning
- Reusable configuration model for agent deployments

## Phase 3: Brokerage Workflows

Focus: team and brokerage capability.

Priorities:

- Enterprise brokerage features
- Team management
- Brokerage dashboards
- Advanced back-office workflows
- CRM integration patterns
- Internal AI assistant planning

## Phase 4: Expansion

Focus: broader real estate and future vertical readiness.

Priorities:

- White-label deployment strategy
- Multi-agent management
- Additional MLS markets
- Multi-brokerage support
- Future industry platform patterns

## Product Guardrails

- Build reusable modules first.
- Configure for Brittany instead of hardcoding for Brittany.
- Do not overbuild full SaaS tenancy before the platform has enough customer pressure to justify it.
- Prioritize recurring customer value and operational efficiency.
