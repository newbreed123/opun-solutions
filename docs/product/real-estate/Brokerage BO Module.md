# Brokerage BO Module

Status: Product Strategy

Owner: Opzix

## Purpose

The Brokerage BO Module supports brokerage back-office and operational workflows when the Real Estate Platform expands beyond individual agent deployments.

BO means back office.

## Goals

- Support team and brokerage operational visibility.
- Provide internal dashboards for leads, appointments, agent activity, and follow-up.
- Prepare for CRM and reporting integrations.
- Keep brokerage workflow design modular until enterprise demand is validated.

## Potential Capabilities

- Multi-agent lead routing
- Team appointment visibility
- Internal AI assistant
- CRM integration
- Operational dashboards
- Reporting exports
- Custom API planning
- Role-based views

## Product Rules

- Do not build full enterprise brokerage management before clear demand.
- Start with configurable workflows that support one team or brokerage implementation.
- Keep individual agent needs separate from brokerage operations.
- Treat enterprise features as a future layer on the same platform foundation.

## Metrics

- Lead routing completion
- Appointment handoff completion
- Agent response time
- Dashboard engagement
- Follow-up status visibility
- CRM sync success
